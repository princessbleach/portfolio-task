01 / ABOUT ME

About
me.

Hi, I’m Zoe! I’m currently pursuing a Games Development BSc at the University of Creative Arts, graduating in 2027. Learning to specialise in technical game audio, I have experience with multiple DAWs and am undergoing Wwise certification.

I enjoy creating soundscapes, designing character voices, implementing audio systems and experimenting with gameplay ideas. I'm also interested in the development tools and workflows that help teams turn creative concepts into playable experiences.

2ANEND

Currently in production, 2ANEND is a rhythm RPG with four playable characters and zones. The project explores musicians' experiences and the struggles faced in the music industry. Developed in Unity with Wwise, with sound effects and music designed in Logic Pro.
